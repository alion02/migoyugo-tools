### Setup
*   Played on an initially empty 8×8 grid.
*   White moves first, black moves second.

### Gameplay
Players alternate placing a piece of their color (**Migo**) on any empty square, subject to one restriction:
*   **Constraint:** You may never create a line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) of pieces of your color **longer than 4**.

### Forming Yugos
When a placement completes **at least one** line of **exactly 4** pieces of the same color:
1.  The placed Migo becomes a **Yugo** (a permanent piece).
2.  All Migos in completed lines are removed from the board (but Yugos remain).
3.  One is added to the player's score for each completed line.

### Winning
*   **Igo (instant win):** Create a line of 4 Yugos.
*   **Wego (endgame):** If the active player has no legal moves, the player with the highest total score wins. Draw if the scores are tied.
